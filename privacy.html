Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2025 · Honestech Mobile Mechanic · New Jersey

This policy explains how Honestech Mobile Mechanic collects, uses, and protects information you provide when requesting service. We keep it simple and straightforward — no selling your data, no marketing lists.

The short version: We only collect what we need to serve you. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. We do not enroll you in marketing messages. Your information is used to schedule, quote, and complete your service.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote or service request, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your service address including street, city, state, and ZIP
  • Vehicle year, make, model, and description of the issue
  • The service you are requesting
  • Your preferred date and time
  • How you heard about us

We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate the website such as security logs or error reports.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Review and respond to your service request
  • Schedule and confirm your appointment
  • Calculate travel and prepare for service
  • Communicate with you directly about your request, estimate, or appointment
  • Process and record payment
  • Maintain business and service records
  • Comply with legal obligations

Google Calendar & Scheduling

When your appointment is confirmed, Honestech may add your appointment details to a private Google Calendar to reserve the service time and avoid scheduling conflicts. A calendar event may include your name, phone number, service address, vehicle, requested service, and confirmed time.

Calendar events are used for internal scheduling only and are not made public. Google processes calendar data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Form Submissions

Quote request forms on this site are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit a form, your information is transmitted to Formspree and delivered to Honestech. Formspree handles form data under its own terms and privacy practices. Honestech does not store your form submissions on this website.

Payment Information

Payments are processed through Stripe. When you make a payment, your card information is handled directly by Stripe. Honestech does not receive or store your full payment card number. Stripe processes payments under its own terms and privacy practices.

Card payments processed through Stripe include a 3% processing fee as disclosed in our Terms and Conditions.

Information Sharing

Honestech does not sell or rent your personal information for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share your information only as necessary with the following service providers that support our operations:

  • Formspree — form submission delivery
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google Calendar — appointment scheduling

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect safety or legal rights, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Calls, Texts & Communication

By providing your phone number, you allow Honestech to call or text you directly about your service request, estimate, appointment, or completed work. Submitting the quote form does not enroll you in automated marketing messages or promotional texts.

Data Retention

We retain service records, payment records, and related information as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. You may request correction or deletion of your information by contacting us directly, subject to records we are required to keep.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. However, no website, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you have concerns about your information, please contact us directly.

Your Choices

You may request to review, correct, or delete information we hold about you, subject to records we reasonably need to keep for completed service, accounting, disputes, or legal obligations. To make a request, contact us by phone or text.

Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. Nothing in this policy limits any consumer right or protection that cannot lawfully be waived under New Jersey law.

For privacy questions or requests, call or text Honestech directly at (732) 937-3734. We are happy to help.
